# Anatomy model attribution

The bundled skeleton, muscular system, brain, heart, lungs, liver, stomach, kidneys, small and large intestines, and trachea are web-ready models derived from BodyParts3D 4.0 and distributed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).

Source and attribution: https://app.humanbodyexplorer.com/model-attribution.html
License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Three.js r128 is bundled for rendering. Google Draco's JavaScript decoder is bundled for compressed mesh decoding. See their respective upstream licenses before redistributing this application.
