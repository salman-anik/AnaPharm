# AnaPharm

**AnaPharm** is an advanced interactive 3D Human Anatomy & Clinical Pharmacopoeia mobile application built with React Native, Expo, and Three.js.

### Features
- **Interactive 3D Human Anatomy:** Complete human figure with realistic body surface / skin, head, neck, torso, arms, hands & fingers, pelvis, thighs, legs, and feet.
- **Dynamic Physiology:** Actively beating heart (BPM control), respiratory lung expansion/contraction, and circulating blood particles.
- **Explosive Disassembly & Holographic X-Ray:** Smooth layered organ inspection with full 360° camera orbit and pinch-to-zoom.
- **AnaPharm Medicine Database:** Integrated clinical pharmacopoeia with generic drug names in English, primary indications & mechanism of action in Bangla, dosage guidelines, side effects, and precautions.
- **Bilingual Medical Dossiers:** Comprehensive English & Bengali clinical information for 20 anatomical structures and top essential medicines.
- **Dual Theme Support:** Dark Mode & Light Mode.
